# Intended purpose and claims policy

## Product category

NeuroMap Kids is an informational parent support tool. It provides structured reflection based on questionnaire answers and generates a parent-friendly PDF report.

## What the product may say

- It helps parents organize observations.
- It highlights patterns in answers.
- It provides non-diagnostic, practical next steps.
- It may suggest when a qualified professional could be helpful.
- It is not a substitute for medical, psychological, educational or therapeutic assessment.

## What the product must not say

- The child has or likely has a disorder.
- The system diagnoses ADHD, autism, anxiety, depression or learning disorder.
- The output is a medical, clinical or therapeutic conclusion.
- The score measures clinical severity.
- The report recommends treatment.
- The system predicts disease, disability or developmental disorder.

## Copy review rule

Every new landing, email, PDF, ad and dashboard copy must be checked against this policy before release. If a sentence would make a parent think the system provides diagnosis, triage, clinical prediction or treatment advice, rewrite it.

## Safer wording examples

Use:
- "possible focus area"
- "pattern worth observing"
- "structured screening summary"
- "parent-friendly explanation"
- "consider consulting a qualified professional"

Avoid:
- "diagnosis"
- "clinical result"
- "risk score"
- "medical recommendation"
- "treatment plan"
- "the child is ADHD/autistic/anxious/depressed"
