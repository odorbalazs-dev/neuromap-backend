# Consumer Rights and Digital Content Checklist

Status: working checklist; local consumer counsel must validate target-country implementation.

## Pre-contract information

- Identify the trader, registered address, contact channels and complaint route.
- Describe Standard and Plus deliverables, total price/currency, taxes, one-time nature, delivery method, expected timing, technical requirements and supported languages.
- State clearly that the output is informational screening, not diagnosis, treatment, emergency advice or professional assessment.
- Present terms and privacy information before payment in a durable/retrievable form.
- Explain complaint handling, conformity remedies and any applicable dispute-resolution information.
- Do not use pre-ticked optional consent, hidden renewal, artificial scarcity, misleading clinical claims or interface pressure that impairs free choice.

## Immediate digital performance and withdrawal

Before starting paid digital performance during a withdrawal period, obtain the exact consent/acknowledgement required by applicable law: an express request to begin, acknowledgement of the consequence for withdrawal rights, and contract confirmation on a durable medium. Do not treat acceptance of general terms alone as sufficient.

The backend stores `digital_performance_requested` and `withdrawal_right_acknowledged`; the post-payment contract confirmation must state package, price, language, legal-document versions, performance request and support/privacy contacts. Counsel must validate wording and exceptions for every targeted jurisdiction.

## Post-contract and delivery

- Send contract confirmation without undue delay and independently of report generation.
- Provide invoice where required.
- Make report delivery retryable and support correction/redelivery without requiring a new purchase.
- If content is not supplied or is non-conforming, provide the remedies required by governing law; do not describe every failure as non-refundable.
- Keep support separate from marketing consent.

## Claims and vulnerable audience

Marketing must not promise diagnosis, medical accuracy, prevention, treatment, school eligibility or guaranteed outcomes. Avoid fear-based targeting and inferred-health advertising audiences. Testimonials and statistics require substantiation and must not imply clinical validation unless independently established.

## Launch evidence

Capture screenshots of each language's offer, consent path, Stripe price/package, success/cancel page and contract email. Reconcile price and entitlement across landing, checkout, webhook, invoice and confirmation. Obtain native legal review for each launched language/country and retain the approved version/date.
